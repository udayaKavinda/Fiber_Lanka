Sustainability

Turning a renewable by-product into responsible growth — for the planet and our communities.

A Renewable Resource

Nurturing Growth Without Depleting the Earth

Coco coir is a by-product of the coconut industry — a renewable resource that would otherwise go to waste. By transforming coconut husk into high-performance growing media, AUN Fiber Lanka helps reduce the horticulture industry's reliance on peat bogs, which take centuries to regenerate.

Our processing sites reuse water through settling and filtration systems, and husk waste that doesn't meet substrate specifications is repurposed as biomass fuel or organic mulch.

Our Commitments

Three Pillars of Responsible Growth

🌍

Environmental

Water recycling, waste-to-energy husk reuse, and reduced-emission logistics across our supply chain.

👥

Social

Fair wages and safe working conditions for our production teams, many of whom are based in rural coconut-growing communities.

💰

Commercial

Long-term contracts with local smallholder husk suppliers, keeping value within Sri Lanka's coconut sector.

Community Impact

Investing Where We Operate

Smallholder Partnerships

We source husk directly from smallholder coconut farmers in the coconut triangle, offering stable pricing and a reliable buyer for a material that would otherwise be discarded.

Local Employment

Our production facilities create skilled and semi-skilled jobs in rural areas, with an emphasis on training and long-term employment.

Water Stewardship

Retting and washing water is treated and recycled through settling ponds before reuse or safe discharge.

Quality Without Compromise

Sustainable sourcing never comes at the cost of consistency — every batch is tested before it leaves our facility.

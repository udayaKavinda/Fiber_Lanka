About AUN Fiber Lanka

Two and a half decades of coir craftsmanship, engineered for the modern grower.

Our Story

From the Coconut Triangle to the World

AUN Fiber Lanka began as a small family-run husking and fiber extraction unit in Sri Lanka's coconut triangle — the fertile region spanning Gampaha, Kurunegala and Puttalam that produces the country's finest coconut husk.

Recognizing the growing global demand for renewable, peat-free growing media, we invested in modern washing, drying and buffering lines to bring our coir up to the specifications required by professional horticulture. Today, AUN Fiber Lanka supplies coco peat, grow bags, coir blocks and natural fiber products to distributors and growers across more than 30 countries.

Every step, from husk collection to container loading, happens within our own facilities — giving us full control over quality, consistency and traceability.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

Early Years

Started as a husk collection and fiber extraction unit supplying local mattress and rope manufacturers.

First Processing Facility

Established our first dedicated coco peat washing and drying facility to serve export markets.

International Expansion

Began direct exports of compressed coco peat blocks and grow bags to Europe and the Middle East.

Quality Certification

Upgraded buffering and testing systems to meet international EC, pH and salinity standards.

Today

Operating multiple production lines, serving over 30 countries with a full range of coir-based products.
